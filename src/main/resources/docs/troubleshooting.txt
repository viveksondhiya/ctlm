INSTANTIATION_IN_PROGRESS - CU-UP

  CU-UP remains in INSTANTIATION_IN_PROGRESS when the deployment acknowledgement is not received from the underlying infrastructure.
  This typically occurs when the Kubernetes pod fails to reach Running state or when the lifecycle management acknowledgement
  timeout is exceeded.

  Recommended action: Check CU-UP pod logs using kubectl logs, verify the deployment status, and inspect lifecycle
  acknowledgement logs in the LCM orchestrator. Verify CU-UP has sufficient CPU and memory resources.

  ---

  INSTANTIATION_IN_PROGRESS - CU-CP

  CU-CP remains pending if the F1 interface initialization fails or if the underlying VNF descriptor validation is rejected.
  CU-CP is the parent CNF and must be fully INSTANTIATED before CU-UP and DU can proceed.

  Recommended action: Verify CU-CP pod is Running, check F1 interface configuration, and review VNF descriptor.

  ---

  INSTANTIATION_IN_PROGRESS - DU

  DU remains pending when the fronthaul connection to CU-CP cannot be established or when the radio unit acknowledgement
  is not received within the expected window.

  Recommended action: Check fronthaul connectivity, verify VLAN configuration, and inspect DU deployment logs.

  ---

  INSTANTIATION_FAILED - General

  A CNF moves to INSTANTIATION_FAILED when the lifecycle management operation exceeds the timeout threshold or when
  a hard failure is detected in the deployment pipeline. This causes the parent NS to also move to INSTANTIATION_FAILED.

  Recommended action: Check audit logs in the LCM controller, inspect Kubernetes events, and retry instantiation
  after resolving the underlying issue.

  ---

  NS stays in INSTANTIATION_IN_PROGRESS

  An NS stays in INSTANTIATION_IN_PROGRESS when one or more of its child CNFs have not yet reached the INSTANTIATED state.
  The NS transitions to INSTANTIATED only when ALL three CNFs (CU-CP, CU-UP, DU) are fully INSTANTIATED.

  Recommended action: Use getCnfStatuses to identify which CNF is blocking, then investigate that specific CNF.
